# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Flower For You aims to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and disciplines.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give actionable feedback about ideas and work, not personal attacks.
- Welcome questions from beginners.
- Respect different disciplines and explain technical decisions clearly.
- Credit creators and respect licenses and intellectual property.
- Keep discussions relevant and collaborative.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith behaviour.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting authorship.
- Knowingly submitting material without redistribution rights.

## Scope and reporting

This Code applies to the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and spaces where someone represents the project.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project owner using the contact method on the repository profile. Include relevant links or evidence. Do not post private reports publicly.

The project owner will review reports and take proportionate action. This may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces.
